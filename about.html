About Isaac

My Playing Career

I grew up playing club soccer in Oklahoma City with Oklahoma Energy, where I was named National Player of the Year. From there I joined the Sporting Kansas City Academy and trained every day in a professional academy environment.

Today I'm a sophomore playing Division 1 soccer at Gonzaga University in Spokane, Washington.

Coaching Experience

I spent one year coaching youth players at Edmond Soccer Club, running practices and helping players build their technical skills and understanding of the game.

I have also spent one year as a private coach, training players one on one and in small groups with plans built around each player's position, strengths, and goals.

My Approach

The best players aren't just talented. They master the basics, work on their weaknesses, and make good decisions quickly. Every session I run has a plan, lots of touches on the ball, game-realistic drills, and honest feedback so players know exactly what to practice next.
