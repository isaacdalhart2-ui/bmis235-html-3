Training Options

Private and small group sessions in Oklahoma City and Edmond for players ages 8 to 18. Every session is 60 minutes.

A full hour built around one player. Best for working on specific skills or getting ready for tryouts.

5-Session Pack: $275

Five one-on-one sessions for $25 less than booking them one at a time. I track progress from week to week so you can see the improvement.

Small Group: $35 per player

Two to four players train together. Great for teammates or siblings, with competitive small-sided games every session.

What We Work On

Ball mastery: first touch, close control, and comfort on both feet.
Finishing: shooting technique, placement, and composure in front of goal.
1v1 moves: beating defenders and changing pace.
Soccer IQ: scanning, positioning, and faster decisions under pressure.

When and Where

Sessions are held at fields around Oklahoma City and Edmond during summer and winter break while I'm home from Gonzaga. The exact field is confirmed when you book.
